The brand lockup. The mark is an F built from three rounded bars: gold stem and top arm (the director), violet middle arm (the crew).

```jsx
<Logo size={22} tagline="mission control" />
<LogoMark size={48} />
<LogoMark size={16} mono style={{ color: 'var(--brand-ink)' }} />
```

- Source file: `assets/logo.svg` (color) and `assets/logo-mono.svg` (currentColor).
- Keep the mark on a flat surface; never place it on imagery or gradients.
- The word is always "Foreman" in the UI face at 600 — never a different typeface, never all-caps.
